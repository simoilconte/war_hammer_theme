# Marine atlas — Gothic revision

Asset: `marines-atlas.png`, 1774 × 887 px, transparent PNG, 8 columns × 4 rows. Generated using the built-in `image_gen` tool; no CLI, API key, external runtime service or image postprocessing. The native UI clips cells from the unchanged atlas. Saved into this project from the generated output; the original remains in the Codex generated-images folder.

Columns: Ultramarines, Blood Angels, Dark Angels, Imperial Fists, Raven Guard, White Scars, Salamanders, Space Wolves. Rows: idle, working, blocked, done. The assignment of a chapter still comes from the pre-existing terminal-address casting map; the image never determines agent status.

Prompt used:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: a production game sprite atlas on a genuinely transparent background, no text. Create exactly 32 separate full-body Warhammer 40,000 loyalist Space Marine sprites, arranged on a perfectly regular EIGHT COLUMN by FOUR ROW grid. Canvas should be 2048px wide by 1024px high, with 256px by 256px square cells. Every cell contains one complete character, no overlap, no cropped limbs, generous transparent margins. Columns left to right: 1 Ultramarines rich cobalt blue armor and gold trim, 2 Blood Angels blood red and gold armor, 3 Dark Angels deep forest green and ivory with small robe, 4 Imperial Fists saturated yellow with black trim, 5 Raven Guard black armor with white shoulder heraldry, 6 White Scars white armor with red lightning markings, 7 Salamanders bright green armor with black and flame orange details, 8 Space Wolves blue-gray armor, yellow shoulder, wolf fur. Consistent character identity, colors and silhouette within each column. ROW 1: idle guard, lowered bolter held across body, facing three-quarter right. ROW 2: working, firing bolter aimed right, small golden muzzle flash. ROW 3: awaiting orders, holding bolter down and raising left hand to helmet comms. ROW 4: mission complete, victorious raised gauntlet and bolter held down. Style: highly detailed hand-painted pixel art inspired by premium 1990s 16-bit strategy game sprites and gothic science-fiction strategy UI, 3/4 perspective, sturdy power-armored human proportions, rounded oversized pauldrons, detailed metal armor plating, chest eagle, illuminated helmet eyes, small chapter insignia, rich saturated colors, bright tiny edge highlights and clear dark pixel outlines. Not chibi, not toy-like, not voxel, not simplified block icons. Precise crisp clusters of pixels and dramatic shaded metal. All characters at exactly the same scale and alignment, anchored on the same baseline inside each cell, about 190px tall in their 256px cell. Transparent canvas throughout: no scenery, no terrain, no floor, no contact shadow, no UI borders, no numbers, no letters, no labels, no watermark. This will be clipped cell-by-cell by a native game UI: a precise regular grid of EIGHT columns and FOUR rows is essential.

The tool returned the exact 2:1 aspect ratio at 1774 × 887 rather than the requested 2048 × 1024. Native rendering normalizes the grid to the chosen cell size, so no raster resize or crop is necessary. All four poses and eight chapter colors were inspected in the generated output; Working, Idle and Done were also observed in the live app. Blocked remains covered by isolated native fixtures.
